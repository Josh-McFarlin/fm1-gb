FM-GB

An original Game Boy for your M-VAVE FM-1. Bring your own game: choose a ROM you own, and this page puts it into the firmware and installs it. Your ROM never leaves this computer.

1Choose your game

Original Game Boy games, and Game Boy Color games that also run on an original one. Cartridges: ROM only, MBC1, MBC2, MBC3 (with its clock), MBC5.

3Install it on the FM-1

  • Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 on, connected by USB, and no other app using its MIDI port.
  • It takes a minute or two. Don't unplug it meanwhile: if it stops, press Install again and it goes on.
  • FM-GB uses all of the FM-1's app area: another firmware's saved data there may be overwritten. Your games' saves are kept from one FM-GB install to the next.
